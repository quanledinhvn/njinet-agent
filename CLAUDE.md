### **FOLLOW THIS WRITING STYLE:**

*   **DO** use clear, simple language.
*   **DO** be concise and information-rich.
*   **DO** use short, powerful sentences.
*   **DO** use active voice; avoid passive voice.
*   **DO** focus on practical, actionable information.
*   **DO** use bulleted lists when writing for social media.
*   **DO** use data and examples to back up your arguments whenever possible.
*   **DO** use "you" and "your" to speak directly to the reader.
*   **AVOID** using em dashes (—). Only use commas, periods, or standard punctuation. If you need to connect ideas, use a period or semicolon; absolutely do not use em dashes.
*   **AVOID** the structure "not only this, but also that."
*   **AVOID** metaphors and clichés.
*   **AVOID** generalizations.
*   **AVOID** introductory phrases like "in summary," "in conclusion," etc.
*   **AVOID** warnings or notes outside the requested content.
*   **AVOID** unnecessary adjectives and adverbs.
*   **AVOID** hashtags.
*   **AVOID** semicolons.
*   **AVOID** markdown formatting.
*   **AVOID** asterisks (*).
*   **AVOID the following words:**
    *   *can, will, just, very, really, essential, likely, perhaps, delve, initiate, beacon, reputable, realm, domain, game-changer, unlock, discover, explore, skyrocket, boom, testament, in a world where, revolutionize, breakthrough, leverage, utilize, dive deep, landscape, intricate, complex, explain, therefore, furthermore, however, tap into, cutting-edge, remarkable, it, it remains to be seen, a glimpse into, in short, to sum up, in conclusion, moreover, enhance, foster, transform*

**IMPORTANT:** Double-check to ensure **there are no em dashes!**
